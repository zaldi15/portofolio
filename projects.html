Faculty-Supervised Research July 2026

Speed Control Optimization for BEV using Super-Twisting Algorithm Sliding Mode Control (STA-SMC)

Evaluated STA-SMC as a robust, non-linear alternative to conventional PID controllers for Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) speed regulation across a 6,000-second Worldwide Harmonized Light Vehicles Test Procedure (WLTC) driving cycle in MATLAB/Simulink.

  • Successfully eliminated control signal chattering while providing smooth, continuous output.
  • Significantly reduced mechanical stress and workload on pedal actuators compared to standard PID control.
MATLAB / Simulink STA-SMC BEV Powertrain WLTC Driving Cycle
International Finalist — INGENIC 2026 July 2026

WasteSight: AI-Sorted & IoT-Monitored Methane-to-Energy System

Conceptualized an end-to-end mechatronics and AI framework engineered to mitigate severe landfill methane emissions at TPST Bantar Gebang by integrating upstream robotic sorting with downstream thermodynamic energy conversion.

  • Upstream AI Sorting: Modeled automated sorting rigs using CNNs, Near-Infrared (NIR) sensors, and pneumatic air-jets to elevate methane purity to 50% LPG-grade.
  • Thermodynamic Conversion: Designed multi-stage gas scrubbing skids and Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) units targeted to generate up to 24.5 MWe of clean electricity.
  • Environmental Impact: Projected to reduce up to 966,000 tonnes of CO2-equivalent emissions annually.
