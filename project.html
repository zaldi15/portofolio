Conference Publication ICSMech 2026 Accepted Paper

Model Predictive Control for Vehicle Speed Based on LiDAR Sensor Data for Autonomous Vehicle Systems

The 2nd International Conference on Smart Mechatronics (ICSMech 2026)

Abstract / Summary: This research focuses on the design and implementation of a Model Predictive Control (MPC) scheme for dynamic vehicle speed regulation in autonomous driving scenarios. By leveraging real-time 3D LiDAR point cloud data for obstacle detection, the system calculates optimal speed trajectories to ensure driving stability and collision avoidance under dynamic environmental constraints.
